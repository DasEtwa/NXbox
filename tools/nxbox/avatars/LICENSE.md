# NXbox original avatar art

These original character portraits were made for NXbox, for owner NSPX, using OpenAI image
generation from the original production brief in `prompts.json`. They are not Nintendo artwork and
do not depict licensed third-party characters, logos, trademarks or Mii designs. No third-party
reference images were supplied.

The PNG masters and derived portrait pixels are made available under the MIT License below. This
permission covers the artwork, not third-party marks or other artwork elsewhere in this repository.
The existing NXbox logo is unchanged.
